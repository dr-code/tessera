## Superpowers (obra/superpowers)

The skills in `skills/` that originate from Superpowers (the folders listed in
`overlays/UPSTREAM.json`) are copies of files from https://github.com/obra/superpowers at the
tag and commit recorded there, plus tessera's additions in `overlays/skills/`. Every generated
`SKILL.md` carries a note naming the upstream tag and commit.
